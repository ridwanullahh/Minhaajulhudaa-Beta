# PRD: Minhaajulhudaa Travels & Tours Platform (`/travels`)

## 1. Overview

A Hajj and Umrah-focused travel platform with a powerful booking engine, dynamic pricing, package management, customer dashboard with LMS for pre-travel education, payment integration, review system, and travel resource library. Designed for pilgrims, travelers, and admin staff.

## 2. Public Pages

### 2.1 Homepage
- Hero with featured Hajj/Umrah packages
- Package categories (Hajj, Umrah, Local tours, International tours)
- Featured packages with pricing
- Why choose us section
- Customer reviews highlights
- Travel guide previews
- Newsletter signup
- Quick quote form

### 2.2 About Us
- Company story and mission
- IATA/licensing info
- Team profiles
- Awards and certifications
- Partner airlines and hotels

### 2.3 Hajj & Umrah Services
- Service packages overview
- Hajj packages (with tiers: standard, deluxe, VIP)
- Umrah packages (year-round, Ramadan special)
- What's included (visa, flight, hotel, transport, guide)
- Pre-departure education (LMS integration)
- Group and individual options

### 2.4 Local & International Tours
- Tour archive with destination filter
- Single tour page: itinerary, inclusions, price, dates
- Booking flow integration

### 2.5 Booking Page
- Package selection
- Date selection (calendar with availability)
- Traveler details (passport info, special needs)
- Add-ons (extra nights, insurance, tours)
- Dynamic pricing display
- Payment options
- Confirmation

### 2.6 Reviews
- Review archive with rating filter
- Verified booking reviews
- Photo and video reviews
- Rating breakdown

### 2.7 Travel Guide & Resources
- Visa requirements by country
- Passport guidance
- Packing checklists
- Hajj/Umrah rituals guide
- Health and safety tips
- Currency and payment abroad
- Emergency contacts

### 2.8 Blog
- Travel tips and experiences
- Destination guides
- Islamic travel perspectives
- Categories and tags

### 2.9 Courses (LMS)
- Pre-travel education courses (Hajj rituals, Umrah guide, Makkah/Madinah history)
- Course archive with level filter
- Single course landing page
- Enrollment (free for bookers, paid for others)

## 3. Admin Panel (CMS)

### 3.1 Dashboard
- Booking summary (today, month, year)
- Revenue analytics
- Upcoming departures
- Pending payments
- Customer inquiries
- Quick actions

### 3.2 Package Management
- Package CRUD (Hajj, Umrah, tours)
- Itinerary builder (day-by-day)
- Inclusions and exclusions
- Pricing tiers
- Availability calendar
- Capacity management
- Photo gallery per package

### 3.3 Booking Management
- Booking pipeline (pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled)
- Manual booking creation
- Booking detail: traveler info, package, dates, payment status
- Document management (passports, visas, tickets)
- Communication log

### 3.4 Payment Management
- Payment tracking per booking
- Installment plans
- Refund processing
- Payment gateway integration (Paystack, etc.)
- Invoice and receipt generation

### 3.5 Dynamic Pricing
- Date-based pricing rules
- Group size discounts
- Early bird pricing
- Seasonal surcharges
- Last-minute deals

### 3.6 Customer Management
- Customer profiles with booking history
- Document vault (encrypted)
- Preferences and notes
- Communication history

### 3.7 Customer Dashboard (User-facing)
- Booked itineraries
- Invoices and payment status
- Documents (tickets, visas, vouchers)
- Pre-travel course access (LMS)
- Communication with agency
- Review submission

### 3.8 Travel Resource Library
- Resource CRUD (articles, checklists, guides)
- Category management
- Downloadable attachments

### 3.9 LMS Management
- Course CRUD for travel education
- Lesson management
- Enrollment tracking
- Progress monitoring
- Certificate issuance

### 3.10 Review Management
- Review moderation
- Response to reviews
- Rating analytics
- Featured review selection

### 3.11 Blog Management
- Article CRUD
- Categories and tags
- Author management

### 3.12 Gallery Management
- Destination photos
- Tour photos
- Customer photos (with permission)

## 4. Collections (Lightbase Schema)

| Collection | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| `travels_pages` | Static page content |
| `travels_packages` | Travel packages |
| `travels_itineraries` | Day-by-day itineraries |
| `travels_bookings` | Booking records |
| `travels_booking_travelers` | Traveler info per booking |
| `travels_payments` | Payment records |
| `travels_customers` | Customer profiles |
| `travels_documents` | Document references (encrypted) |
| `travels_pricing_rules` | Dynamic pricing configs |

| `travels_lessons` | Course lessons |
| `travels_enrollments` | Course enrollments |
| `travels_gallery` | Gallery media |
| `travels_inquiries` | Customer inquiries |
| `travels_settings` | Platform settings |
